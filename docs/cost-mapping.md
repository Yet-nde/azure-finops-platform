**Business Cost Mapping** <br />
**Purpose** <br />
Azure Cost Management exposes expenditure using technical Azure service terminology. This project introduces a business-friendly reporting layer for non-technical stakeholders.

**Proposed Categories**
|Azure Service | Business Category|
| --- | --- |
|Virtual Machines | Servers|
|Storage Accounts | Storage|
|Azure SQL Database | Databases|
|App Service | Applications|
|Azure Functions | Automation|
|Logic Apps | Automation|
|Log Analytics | Monitoring|
|Azure Monitor | Monitoring|
|Key Vault | Security|

**Principles** <br />
Azure-native service information will be preserved for technical analysis. <br />

Business categories provide an additional reporting abstraction without replacing the underlying Azure service information.
